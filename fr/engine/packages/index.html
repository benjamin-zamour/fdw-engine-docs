Packages

Chaque package (moteur), son rôle, sa visibilité et les domaines qu’il sert. Documenté ne veut pas dire stable : la visibilité dit qui peut en dépendre. Les fiches des packages sont en anglais.

Package Rôle Visibilité Domaines
engine-animation Skeletons, clips, poses, blends and animation graphs, on the CPU. Public Animation
engine-assets Images, textures, meshes, materials and models: their CPU data and their realizations on a backend. Public Ressources, assets et formats
engine-audio The abstract audio API: engines, buses, clips, voices, a listener, sound effects, music and spatial sounds. Public Audio
engine-audio-native The audio engine of the native platforms for @fdw/engine-audio, on miniaudio. Plateforme Audio
engine-audio-web The audio engine of a page for @fdw/engine-audio, on WebAudio. Plateforme Audio
engine-backend The abstract rendering API: the contract between the engine and its backends. Public Rendu
engine-behavior Behavior graphs: events, the flow of execution, values computed on demand, variables and actions. Public Comportements
engine-camera Camera operators driven by the actions of @fdw/engine-input: orbit, fly, first and third person. Public Entrées et caméras
engine-core The frontend of the engine: meshes, materials, the renderer and the render graph, independent of the backend. Public Rendu, Matériaux et shaders, Scène
engine-d3d12 The Direct3D 12 backend, in Node on Windows, through a native library. Plateforme Rendu
engine-dataviz Values on a model and in time: color scales and their legend, the time cursor and its bar, probes and their curves, over an abstract time axis and value provider. Public Texte, dessin et inspection, Procédural et simulation
engine-draw Lines and texts drawn by the renderer, in the scene or at a constant size on the screen. Public Texte, dessin et inspection
engine-editing The editing tools: selection, gizmos, commands, undo and redo, the events of the scene. Public Scène, Entrées et caméras
engine-fluids Fluids simulated on the GPU in compute graphs: shallow water, smoke, liquids of particles. Public Procédural et simulation
engine-geometry Volumes, intersections and spatial queries, in pure TypeScript. Public Géométrie, Scène
engine-gltf The glTF 2.0 loader and writer: scenes, materials, animations, extensions and decoders. Public Ressources, assets et formats
engine-graph The graph core shared by the material, animation and behavior graphs and their editors. Public Matériaux et shaders, Animation, Comportements, Édition des graphs
engine-graph-editing The editing of the graphs on the side of the engine: sessions, previews and commands, without the DOM. Interne Édition des graphs
engine-image The image encoders: PNG, JPEG, OpenEXR and Radiance HDR files from pixels or from a texture. Public Ressources, assets et formats
engine-input The keyboard, the pointer and the gamepads read the same way everywhere, and named actions bound to them. Public Entrées et caméras
engine-input-native The input source of a native window for @fdw/engine-input, on SDL3. Plateforme Entrées et caméras
engine-input-web The input source of a page for @fdw/engine-input: an element or the window, the Gamepad API. Plateforme Entrées et caméras
engine-ktx2 The KTX2 codec: compressed textures with their levels, for @fdw/engine-assets. Public Ressources, assets et formats
engine-measure Measures on the mesh of a model with their precision: distances, thicknesses, angles, radii. Public Texte, dessin et inspection, Géométrie
engine-native The engine in Node: native windows, a render loop, surfaces and the native addon. Plateforme Rendu
engine-navigation Navigation meshes, paths, agents, obstacles and avoidance. Public Navigation
engine-noise The noises of the material graph in TypeScript, the same values on the CPU and the GPU. Public Procédural et simulation, Matériaux et shaders
engine-obj The loader of Wavefront OBJ files and their MTL materials. Public Ressources, assets et formats
engine-occlusion Occlusion culling: the objects hidden behind those of the previous frames are not drawn. Public Rendu
engine-ocean An animated sea on every backend, and the height of its water for the physics. Public Procédural et simulation
engine-physics The abstract physics API and its binding to the scene: worlds, bodies, colliders, joints, queries, characters. Public Physique
engine-physics-rapier The physics API of @fdw/engine-physics on Rapier, compiled to WebAssembly. Public Physique
engine-pivot The pivot model and the engine, both ways: pivot models loaded as models, scenes written as pivot models. Public Ressources, assets et formats
engine-ragdoll Ragdolls: bodies on the bones of a skeleton, linked by joints, their pose read back for the skinning. Public Physique, Animation
engine-raytracing Ray tracing on the backends with ray queries: shadows, reflections, ambient occlusion and a path tracer. Public Ray tracing
engine-resources The resource system: asynchronous loading, caching and lifetimes, independent of the backend. Public Ressources, assets et formats
engine-resources-native The Node extensions of @fdw/engine-resources: files with notifications, image decoding, worker threads. Plateforme Ressources, assets et formats
engine-resources-web The web extensions of @fdw/engine-resources: image decoding, file sources, hot reload, workers. Plateforme Ressources, assets et formats
engine-runtime The contract between a running scene and what runs it: a scene made by a factory from a backend, a surface and a context, in a page, a native window or the editor. Public Scène, Rendu
engine-scene The scene: entities in a hierarchy, components, systems and their scheduler, spatial queries. Public Scène
engine-scene-io Saving and loading scenes: levels, prefabs and the files of other tools. Public Scène, Ressources, assets et formats
engine-shader The material graph: typed nodes compiled to WGSL, GLSL ES, SPIR-V and HLSL. Public Matériaux et shaders
engine-skinning Skinning on the GPU: in a compute pass on WebGPU and Vulkan, in the vertex stage on WebGL2. Public Animation
engine-tessellation Tessellation without hardware tessellation: patches subdivided by their size on the screen, displaced. Public Rendu, Géométrie
engine-text Fonts, text layout and the MSDF of the glyphs, without the GPU. Public Texte, dessin et inspection
engine-vulkan The Vulkan 1.3 backend, in Node, through a native library. Plateforme Rendu
engine-webgl2 The WebGL2 backend, for the browsers without WebGPU. Plateforme Rendu
engine-webgpu The WebGPU backend, in the browser and on Dawn. Plateforme Rendu

Généré depuis le dépôt par tools/docs/catalog.mjs : le README, la description et le champ fdw de chaque package.json, ses tags Nx.
