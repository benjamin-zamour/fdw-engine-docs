MeasureMesh

Ce contenu n’est pas encore disponible dans votre langue.

Defined in: engine/engine-measure/src/mesh.ts:65

A mesh as the measures see it, in world units: its vertices welded (the GPU meshes split them by normal), its triangles with their neighbours across each edge, a BVH for the rays. Built once per mesh and placement.

The precision of what is measured on it is estimated from its facets: the vertices are taken to lie on the true surface (what a tessellation does), and the chord of a facet to deviate from it by its sag, w θ / 8 for a facet of width w whose normal turns by θ towards a smooth neighbour (an arc of radius w / θ).
