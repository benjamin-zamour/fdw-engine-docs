Source geometry/pivot-fcstd

The reader of FreeCAD’s documents (.FCStd) of the pivot model (@fdw/pivot): the shapes shown, as exact B-reps; the groups, parts and bodies as nodes; the links as instances; the colors of the views; and the construction history as the feature tree of the model. The first format with a history. Design: design/p22-pivot.md (in French), step 5; the tutorial « Reading a FreeCAD document » of the documentation.

It depends on @fdw/pivot, @fdw/capabilities and @fdw/pivot-brep (each shape of a document is a BREP file): TypeScript only, no FreeCAD, no OpenCascade; the archive is inflated by DecompressionStream, which the browsers and Node have. A writer is out of its scope for now.

Usage

Section titled “Usage”

In the engine, installPivot(store, registry, { kernel }) of @fdw/engine-pivot loads .FCStd files as models; the viewer of the editor opens them.

What it reads

Section titled “What it reads”
  • The archive: Document.xml (the objects, their types and properties), GuiDocument.xml if present (their views), the shapes in BREP (Box.Shape.brp), the colors in binary files (ShapeAppearance of FreeCAD 1.x; DiffuseColor before).
  • The tree: an object with a Group (an App::Part, a PartDesign::Body, a group) is a node, its children under it, its placement its transform if it has one (a part, a body). The origins and their planes and axes are not parts of the model. A shape shown is a node and its B-reps, read by placeBrepShape of @fdw/pivot-brep: the shape’s own placement is its location. A link (App::Link) is an instance of a prototype of what it links, its placement replacing that of the object (or composed with it, LinkTransform). The labels name the nodes; the names of FreeCAD are their source keys.
  • The visibility: of the view if the file has one, of the document otherwise. A hidden shape (a step of the construction: the box before its cut, the pad before its pocket) is not read, unless hidden: true, which reads it as a hidden node.
  • The colors: one for a shape, or one a face, and the transparency; sRGB in FreeCAD, linear in the pivot.
  • The history: every object but the origins is a feature, in the order of the document: its type (PartDesign::Pad), its label, its parameters by property ({ type, value }: numbers, booleans, strings, links, placements, vectors, colors; the element as JSON for the others, the geometry of a sketch for one), its expressions, whether it is suppressed, the B-reps it produced. Its content is the business of the CAD editing (P13).
  • The document: its label names the model; its author, company, comment, license, dates are properties of the model. FreeCAD works in millimeters, Z up.

What it says

Section titled “What it says”

Never a silent loss: the hidden shapes not read (with their count), the edges and wires outside of a face (a sketch shown), the links to another document, the arrays of links, a shape that cannot be read. A file that is not a FreeCAD document (not a zip, no Document.xml) is a PivotError (invalid).

Public API

Section titled “Public API”
Export Description
registerFcstd, fcstdPivotManifest, fcstdPivotPlugin The plugin fdw.fcstd (pivot.reader, .fcstd), in a registry
fcstdPivotReader, FcstdReaderOptions, fcstdStage The reader; whether the hidden shapes are read

placementMatrix A placement of FreeCAD as a matrix, column-major

parseXml, XmlElement, XmlError A small XML parser: elements, attributes, text

Limits

Section titled “Limits”

The links to other documents and the arrays of links are not read (and said); the colors of a link that overrides those of its object, the materials, the per-face colors of a compound placed several times, the expressions are not evaluated (their values are those FreeCAD saved). A file of FreeCAD before 0.19, without Visibility in its document and without a view, shows every shape.

Testing

Section titled “Testing”

npx vitest run --config geometry/pivot-fcstd/vitest.config.mts, on documents written by FreeCAD 1.1 (scripts/make-documents.py, in a session with its interface, offscreen) beside what FreeCAD says of them (fixtures/*.expected.json): the global placement, the volume (measured by OpenCascade) and the faces of each shape shown, the link and its placement; the tree, the colors by object and by face, the transparency; the feature tree (types, parameters, links, expressions, the B-reps produced); the hidden shapes when asked; the clamp of the documentation, every body tessellated by the basic kernel.
