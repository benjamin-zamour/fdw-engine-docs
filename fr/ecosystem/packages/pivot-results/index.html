Source conversion/pivot-results

The results of a simulation in the pivot model (@fdw/pivot): the meshes they are carried by, their fields, their steps and cases, loaded on demand. Design: specs/design/p31-simulation.md (in French), steps 1 and 8 (the correspondence of two supports); the tutorial « Reading simulation results » of the documentation.

Pure TypeScript, without GPU and without the engine: it depends only on @fdw/pivot. The results are an extension of the pivot, in the namespace simulation.results: extension resources that the core of the pivot keeps without reading them, read by the readers of results (@fdw/pivot-vtk) and, later, by the simulation profile of the viewer.

What a result is

Section titled “What a result is”
  • A result mesh (ResultMesh): points and cells, as read, without remeshing; the cell types and the order of their points are those of VTK (CellType), the numbers the solver gave its points and cells kept when the file has them (pointIds, cellIds), a link to the part of the model by its source key. Distinct from the meshes shown.
  • A field (ResultField): a name, a kind (scalar, vector, tensor, symmetric tensor, array), its components, a unit declared with the data (absent if the file does not say it), a location kept as read: at the points, constant in the cells, at the integration points.
  • An axis (ResultAxis): the steps of a transient result, load cases, eigenmodes or frequencies, each with its value.
  • The values of a field at a step (FieldValues): a resource of their own, deferred until read; NaN where a value is missing, never interpolated.
  • A result set (ResultSet): the axis, the meshes, the fields and the ids of their values by step. A node in the tree shows each mesh (its extension names the set and the mesh) and draws its skin (skinMesh, a mesh of the pivot): a viewer that does not understand results still shows their support.

Public API

Section titled “Public API”
Export Description
ResultMesh, ResultField, ResultSet, ResultAxis, FieldValues The data of the namespace resultsNamespace
CellType, cellTypeName, cellDimension, cellSize The cell types, by the numbers of VTK
resultSets, resultMesh, resultField, cellPoints, isResultResource The results of a model, found
ResultsBuilder Adds the results to a model being built (PivotBuilder): meshes, fields, values read or deferred
validateResults, meshProblems, valuesProblem The meshes, the fields and their values checked; what does not fit dropped and reported
FieldCache, registryLoader The values read on demand, the least recently used evicted past a budget of bytes; prefetching
scalarOf, fieldRange, entryOf A component, a magnitude, a von Mises stress; the extremes and where they are, the missing counted
resultSkin, skinMesh The outer surface of a mesh, to draw it: each triangle with its cell, each vertex with its point; as a mesh of the pivot
matchSupports, SupportMatch, SupportMismatch Whether two result meshes are one support (points within a tolerance, cells), by the solver’s numbers or the order; why not

Limits

Section titled “Limits”

A mesh that changes from one step to the next (remeshing, eroded elements) is not represented: one mesh a part for the whole axis. Complex fields (amplitude and phase) are two fields. The skin draws quadratic cells by their corners, and says how many; it skips the polyhedra and the cell types it does not know, kept in the mesh as read.

Testing

Section titled “Testing”

npx vitest run --config conversion/pivot-results/vitest.config.mts: a result set built, kept by validateModel, checked; meshes and values that do not fit dropped and reported; the components, magnitudes and von Mises stresses; the skin of every 3D cell turned outwards, the shared faces left out; the cache: one reading for concurrent requests, the eviction, the values in the model, the values that do not fit, an aborted request.
