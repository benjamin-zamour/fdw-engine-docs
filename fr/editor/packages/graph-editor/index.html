The editor of graphs in the page, in React, shared by the graph plugins of the editor.
Product Editor
Visibility Internal: Used by the products of the workspace; its API can change without notice.
Domains Graph editing, The editor
Dependencies editor-ui, engine-core, engine-graph, engine-graph-editing

API API reference
Source editor/graph-editor

The editor of graphs of FdwEngine, in the page (P19 of design/roadmap.md, design in design/p19-graph-editor.md, in French): the canvas, the search, the inspector, the panel the plugins of the graphs build on, the client of the engine; and ./engine, the part that runs with the engine. The plugins: materials, animations, behaviors.

  • GraphCanvas: the nodes in HTML, the wires in SVG, at the geometry of engine-graph (nodeSize, portPosition: no measure of the page). The view moves by the middle or the right button, zooms at the wheel; selection by click (Shift adds, Ctrl toggles) and rectangle; nodes, notes and groups dragged; wires dragged between ports (those that fit grow, by the hint of the kind), into the void the search filtered on the nodes that take them; Alt+click disconnects. The keys: Delete, Ctrl+C, X, V, D, A, Z, Y, F, Home, Space or Tab (search), Ctrl+F (find), N (note), Ctrl+G and Ctrl+Shift+G (group); the others go to the editor (the keys of the plugins). The GraphOverlay shows the running graph: classes, badges, weights, values, markers, active wires.
  • NodeSearch: by words, in the titles first, then categories, details, keywords. NodeInspector: the label, the parameters by their kind (a choice, a number, a vector by component, JSON), the values of the inputs not connected, the problems.
  • GraphPanel: the bar (the graph shown among those of the scene, unless targets={false}; new, open and save through the host, or by the plugin, onNew, onOpenFile, onSaved, for documents in tabs; undo and redo, arrange, frame, the function shown, the problems counted, live), the canvas or a view of the plugin (override), the column of tabs (those of the plugin, Node, Problems), the messages of the engine.
  • GraphClient: a session in the engine (the extension fdw.graphs), opened on a graph of the scene, a file or a new graph, again when the engine restarts; what the engine tells, as a store (useGraphClient); the preview of a material by its PreviewSettings (preview(settings), none to stop it).
  • graphsExtension (./engine): the GraphWorkspace of @fdw/engine-graph-editing on the graphs of the scene shown (read again twice a second), its previews drawn by the backend of the engine, their models read and the materials converted by its pivot (readModel, pivot of the context of the extensions); it holds the scene while a debugger stops it.
  • ./testing: renderGraphEditor (the shell, its engine a workspace on targets; in a profile, with the options of the workspace), stubGraphDom (PointerEvent and canvases for jsdom).

Tested in jsdom (src/GraphCanvas.spec.tsx): the drawing, the moves, the connections and their hints, the search for a wire dropped, copy and paste, groups and notes, the overlay.
