Source editor/project

The project of the applications built on the engine (the game editor, the material editor, the design review, the render studio): a folder, its manifest, the sources of its assets with, beside each, its metadata, and the catalog derived from them. Pure TypeScript, without the GPU, without the editor: the folder is any source that lists, writes and removes. Design: specs/design/p20-game-editor.md §5 and §12 (in French). Tutorial: Projects and the catalog in the documentation.

  • The folder: ProjectFolder, a ResourceSource with list, write and remove. FileSource of @fdw/engine-resources-native on the desktop, DirectorySource of @fdw/engine-resources-web on the web (the File System Access API), MemoryFolder here for tests and projects built in code.
  • The manifest, fdw-project.json at the root: its format and version, the name, the paths not scanned (ignore), the settings of the applications. The names starting with a dot (.git, .fdw) are never assets.
  • A GUID per asset, in its metadata beside its source (trees/oak.glb.meta): readable JSON, versioned with the source; its type, the settings of its import, its dependencies. The identity of an asset is its GUID, not its path.
  • The catalog, derived from the metadata, never the source of truth: scanned at the opening and by refresh, the metadata read only when their revision changed (the cache in .fdw/catalog.json, ignored by version control). It is a CatalogSource of @fdw/engine-resources: mounted under asset, asset:<guid> reads the source wherever it is; a move (move) keeps the GUID and reloads what uses it.
  • Nothing in silence: a source without metadata gets one (meta-created) or is reported (missing-meta); metadata without its source (orphan-meta), invalid (invalid-meta); two sources with the same GUID (duplicate-guid: a copy with its metadata), the source that had it kept (at the opening, the first by path), the others out of the catalog until regenerateGuid. An action refused throws a ProjectError with a code, nothing written.
  • The references of the scenes: asset:<guid> anywhere in the data (assetReferences, the format need not be known); missingAssets(scene) lists those the catalog does not have; rewriteReferences(scene, path) changes the references by path into references by GUID, each change listed.
  • Libraries: mount(name, folder) adds the assets of another folder under @<name>/ (a library of materials, the store of the reviews, the destinations of the renders), their GUIDs unique with those of the project.

Out of its scope: the cooking of the assets (P20, step 5), the editor and its asset browser, the watching of a whole folder (a scan is explicit: refresh).

Export Description

ProjectManifest, AssetMeta, readManifest, readMeta, encodeJson The formats of the manifest and of the metadata
createGuid, isGuid, typeOf, defaultTypes The GUIDs (UUID v4), the types of assets by the end of their names

AssetReference, assetReferences, assetUri, parseAssetUri, mapStrings The asset: references in data
RewrittenReference A reference by path changed into a reference by GUID
