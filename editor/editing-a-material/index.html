Editing a material

The materials of the engine are graphs (Material graphs). The editor edits them with the mouse, in the Material graph panel: the same editor of graphs as the animations and the behaviors, each kind of graph a plugin of the editor. This tutorial changes the wood of the gallery of procedural materials, while it is drawn.

The editor below shows the gallery, the panel opened beside the viewport (View → Material graph, or Graphs: Edit the materials in the palette). Nothing is kept when the page is left.

Open the editor in a page of its own

1. The graph of the scene

Section titled “1. The graph of the scene”

The list at the left of the bar has the materials of the scene shown: the gallery gives each object’s (wood, marble, rock, lava, bricks, tiles, baked rock), the level its own. The first one opens by itself; live says that each change that compiles is drawn in the scene at once, the material made again with the values of the old one.

The canvas moves like the viewport:

Input What it does
Middle or right button, dragged Moves the view
Wheel Zooms around the pointer
F, the frame button Frames the selection, or the whole graph
Click, Shift+click, Ctrl+click Selects, adds, toggles
Drag on the background Selects in a rectangle
Drag on a node Moves the selection
Space or Tab Searches a node to add, at the pointer
Ctrl+F Finds a node of the graph
Delete Removes the selection (nodes, wires, notes)
Ctrl+C, X, V, D Copy, cut, paste, duplicate
Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+Y Undo, redo (also in the bar)
N, Ctrl+G A note at the pointer, a group of the selection

The keys go to the canvas once it is clicked. The arrangement button places the nodes again in columns, from the inputs to the output: the materials made in code have no positions, and are arranged so when opened.

2. Nodes and wires

Section titled “2. Nodes and wires”

A node has its inputs on the left, its outputs on the right, each with its type: f32, vec3<f32>… An input not connected shows its value, in gray. Drag from a port to another to connect them: while dragging, the ports that take the wire grow. The engine checks the connection against the graph: a vec4<f32> into a vec3<f32> is refused, and the message of the validation shows at the bottom of the canvas. The only conversion is a scalar into a vector.

Alt+click on a port removes its wires. A wire dropped in the void opens the search, filtered on the nodes that take it: the node chosen is added there, connected.

Try it on the wood:

  1. Press Space over the canvas, type smoothstep, Enter.
  2. Drag the output of a mul node into its x input, and its output into a mix of the color. The sphere of the preview and the capsule of the gallery change.
  3. Ctrl+Z undoes the change; each one is a command of the history of the graph (P15), the labels in the tooltips of the bar.

3. The inspector and the problems

Section titled “3. The inspector and the problems”

The column at the right of the panel has tabs:

  • Node: the node selected, its label, its parameters (the type of a uniform among the types of the shaders) and the values of its inputs not connected, a field per component for the vectors. A value is applied by Enter, or by leaving the field.
  • Problems: the errors and warnings of the graph; a click frames the node. A node in error has a red border, its message in its tooltip. The bar counts them.

A graph with an error is not drawn: the scene keeps the last material that compiled, until the graph compiles again.

4. The preview and the code

Section titled “4. The preview and the code”
  • Preview: the material alone, on a sphere, a cube or a plane, drawn by the engine into a texture and read back, recompiled at each change. With Animate, it plays the time of the engine (the lava flows). A material of a file or a new one, without a scene, is seen there.
  • Code: what the engine draws, generated for its backend (WGSL on WebGPU, GLSL ES on WebGL2, HLSL on Vulkan), with the time of the compilation. Its source map links the nodes to the lines: the lines of the node selected stand out, a click on a line selects its node. The nodes laid by a macro (the octaves of a fbm) are those of the macro.

5. Files, notes and groups

Section titled “5. Files, notes and groups”
  • New, Open, Save (the bar): a new material, a .material.json of the user, the graph saved as JSON. A graph saved is a resource of the engine (MaterialGraphType), loaded by a store.
  • N lays a note at the pointer: double-click it to write in it. Ctrl+G groups the nodes selected in a frame, moved with them; double-click its title to rename it, Ctrl+Shift+G ungroups.
  • Notes, groups and positions are saved in the document (layout), ignored by the compiler.

The graph is edited in the engine, which may run in another process: the panel shows its projection and sends commands, as the outliner does for the level. Debugging a behavior uses the same editor on the mind of the enemies of the game.
