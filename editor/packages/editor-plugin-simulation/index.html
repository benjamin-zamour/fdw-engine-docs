@fdw/editor-plugin-simulation

Show the results of a simulation on the viewer: the results manager, the contours and their legend, the deformation, the time, the probes and their curves, the cuts and the iso-surfaces, the glyphs and the streamlines, the volume.
Product Editor
Visibility Public: Meant to be used by applications and plugins.
Domains The editor, Procedural and simulation
Dependencies editor-ui, engine-backend, engine-core, engine-dataviz, engine-draw, engine-geometry, engine-scene, engine-shader, engine-text, pivot, pivot-results, simulation-embed
Dependents None
Tests test-unit (npx nx run editor-plugin-simulation:<target>, the families of the CI in Testing)
API API reference
Source editor/plugins/editor-plugin-simulation

The simulation plugin (P31, steps 3 to 10): the results of a simulation on the viewer. It adds the Results panel (the fields of the file, the value drawn, the step, what the steps are, the deformation), the Contours panel (the palette, the bands, the linear or logarithmic scale, the range fitted or fixed, the smoothing) the Time panel (the steps played, step by step, their speed and loop; a mode swinging) the Probes panel (a quantity at a node or a cell, its value at the step and its extremes over the steps) the Cut and iso panel (a cut across an axis, the outer surface hidden, iso-surfaces at levels, iso-lines) the Glyphs and streamlines panel (arrows of a vector, streamlines from seeds as lines or ribbons), the Volume panel (the field seen through: its resolution, its opacity along the range, its density), the Derived fields panel (a number of a tensor, a formula, a gradient, a difference, each with its formula; those refused and why) and the Compare panel (another step or field side by side in the second view, in the same colors, or their difference), the field, the step, the range and the factor of the deformation in the status bar, and the legend, the time bar, the probes and their curves over the view. Design: specs/design/p31-simulation.md (in French); the tutorials « Showing simulation results », « Deforming and playing simulation results », « Probing simulation results », « Cutting simulation results », « Following the flow in simulation results », « Comparing simulation results », « Seeing through simulation results » and « Embedding simulation results » of the documentation.

The results are read by the conversion (@fdw/pivot-vtk, registered in the engine of the editor by default) into the pivot (@fdw/pivot-results): a result file opens as a model, each result mesh a node drawing its skin. Nothing is computed in the page: the part of the plugin in the engine (simulationExtension) reads the values of a field at a step on demand, writes in each vertex of the skin the position of its value in the range of the color scale of @fdw/engine-dataviz, and draws the nodes with a material that reads the palette per pixel; the legend is drawn over each view.

Its rules, those of P31: a field at the cells is constant by cell by default, averaged at the points only on request, and said so; a missing value is never filled in (a cell without a value stays in the color of the missing values, whatever the smoothing; a point without a displacement stays in place, counted); values out of a fixed range are drawn in the colors below and above, never an end of the palette; a field without a unit says so; the factor of a deformation is always written (under the legend, in the status bar), 1 the true scale, fitted only on request to a round number; the time is always on a step, never between two, and the reading waits for a step before the next (the next ones read ahead).

The deformation: a vector at the points (a displacement) moves the vertices of the contours on the GPU, times the factor (positionOffset of the material); the bounds of the nodes follow; the mesh undeformed is drawn as a wire (the edges of its faces, without their diagonals) on request. The time: a TimeCursor of @fdw/engine-dataviz on the steps of the set (timeAxisOf), advanced by the frames of the engine; the steps of a series may be said to be modes (or cases) when the reader could not tell, then a mode swings between its two signs, slowed down to a cycle a second, its amplitude said to have no meaning.

The probes: ResultsValues, the ValueProvider of @fdw/engine-dataviz on the fields of a set (a quantity a number of a field, stress:von-mises; read by the cache of the engine at the steps of the axis), read by readProbes at the step shown, their series over the steps drawn by CurveDrawing over the view (the first four), their marks by ProbeDrawing on the mesh, deformed when it is. A probe reads one entry of the field as computed: a node a field at the points, a cell a field by cell, by the numbers of the solver (pointIds, cellIds) or the index; a node on a field by cell has no value and says why, never an average. A time between two steps has no value.

The cut and the iso-surfaces (extraction.ts): every surface drawn is an ExtractedSurface, each vertex on a segment between two points of the mesh at a weight, each triangle in a cell, so that a field at the points is read at a vertex by the linear interpolation of its cell and a field by cell stays constant on it. A cut is a plane across an axis: clipSurface keeps the skin on its side, sliceSurface the slice through the 3D cells; isoSurface crosses the 3D cells where values at the points are a level (marching tetrahedra: a hexahedron split in six around its diagonal from 0 to 6, a wedge in three, a pyramid in two, a quadratic cell by its corners), a cell with a point without a value left out and counted; isoLines crosses the triangles of a surface at the edges of the bands. They are merged in one mesh drawn by the material of the contours, deformed with them. A field by cell is constant on a slice, averaged at the points (and said) for its iso-surfaces; iso-lines need values at the points.

The glyphs and the streamlines (flow.ts): a CellLocator finds the cell around a point (the tetrahedra of the 3D cells in a regular grid of buckets) and its barycentric weights, so that a field at the points is read anywhere by the linear interpolation of its cell, a field by cell as read (valuesAtLocations, missing when a corner that weighs is). glyphBases places a bounded number of glyphs (points of the mesh one in every few, a regular grid in the cells, a grid on the plane of the cut; none on the side a cut removes), glyphArrows makes their arrows along the vector, the longest one spacing long, the others by their magnitude or all as long; a place without a vector has no arrow, counted. streamlines integrates from seeds (lineSeeds, planeGrid) by fourth order Runge–Kutta along the direction of the field, by steps of a quarter of a cell, both ways or one; a line stops where it leaves the cells, where the speed vanishes, where a value is missing, or at its length, each counted. ribbons turns a ribbon along each line by half the vorticity along it (the gradient of the field in each tetrahedron). Arrows and ribbons are meshes of the contours (colored by the field shown, deformed by the material), the lines are lines of @fdw/engine-draw in the colors of the scale.

The derived fields (derived.ts): DerivedFields over the cache of the engine adds to each set the fields defined (DerivedDefinition, kept with the layout), each with an id of its own (derived: and its name) and its formula (formulaOf), so that the contours, the probes, the cuts and the glyphs read them as the others; computed at each step asked from the values of their fields, never stored (the last ones kept). A number of a tensor (principalValues, the eigenvalues of its symmetric part in closed form; Tresca, the pressure), a formula (parseFormula: numbers, fields and their components, + - * / ^, functions; scalar, its unit only if given), a gradient (cellGradients: by cell, the linear interpolation of its tetrahedra averaged by their volumes), a difference with a step. A definition that cannot be computed is refused, and why is said: a field missing, a formula that does not read, a cell field with a point field, another unit (never converted), two meshes that do not correspond (matchSupports of @fdw/pivot-results: the same points and cells, by the numbers of the solver or the order); a missing value stays missing.

The comparison: the field shown with another step (or another field), side by side in the second view (viewport.2), in the colors of one scale fitted to both sides, each legend saying its side: the engine gives the nodes of the results a layer the second view leaves out (hiddenLayers), and draws the other side there (items(view)), deformed by its own displacement; or their difference, a derived field, refused when their supports do not correspond.

The volume (volume.ts): volumeSamples lays a regular grid of cubic cells over the 3D cells (Resolution along the longest side, centered), each sample placed in the cells by the CellLocator; volumeValues reads a field there, at the points by the linear interpolation of the cell, by cell as read, nothing outside the cells, missing where a point that weighs has no value — a resampling said in the notes, never the values read. volumeAtlas lays the slices side by side in a 2D texture (the position of each value in the range, premultiplied by its coverage; missing; out of range), transferImage the palette of the scale with an opacity along its range (opacityAt, opacityPresets) on a row. VolumeMaterial (volumeGraph) draws them on the box of the grid, by a march along the ray of the camera in the coordinates of the grid, two slices blended, emitted and absorbed front to back; a back face marches only from a camera inside the box; the side a cut removes is not seen through (gridPlane).

The embedded viewer (api.ts, step 10): serveSimulationApi(runtime, channel) serves the API of @fdw/simulation-embed on the runtime of the shell, on any channel (the web application serves it to its parent with ?api, in the simulation-embedded profile of @fdw/editor-ui). Each command is done by the plugin as its panels would (the command fdw.simulation.api, not in the palette, takes a SimulationAction) and answered once the engine has drawn what it posted (the shown of the same display), with the names of the fields and not their ids (shownForApi): the quantity, the step and its label, the ranges, the entries without a value, the notes, the factor of the deformation. A field, a step or a component that does not exist, a deformation without a displacement, a difference refused are rejected with their reason. The probes answer their value written, the number as computed and its unit (number, unit of ProbeSummary, probeForApi). The events (simulationEvents): the results, what is drawn, the time, the probes, the errors.

Limits

Section titled “Limits”

The values at the integration points, the 1D cells, and the quadratic cells (drawn by their corners) are said under the Contours panel, not drawn as the data is. The normals of a deformed mesh are those of the mesh as read; the outline of the selection follows the mesh undeformed. A series opened from a dialog needs the files of its steps beside it; a probe is placed by its number or at an extreme, not by a click on the model. The extraction runs on the CPU in the engine, at each step shown (the compute graph of P31 §4 is to come); a cut is along an axis, and the iso-surfaces are not cut; the iso-lines do not swing with a mode; a hexahedron of an unstructured mesh may split a face shared with its neighbour by the other diagonal (a crack in an iso-surface across it). The glyphs and the streamlines are computed on the CPU in the engine, at each step shown (not on the GPU nor in a worker); the seeds are on a line or a plane across an axis, not on the selection; a streamline follows the field of one step (no pathline through the steps); the streamlines as lines do not swing with a mode; streamlines and grid glyphs need 3D cells. The derived fields are computed on the CPU in the engine (not by a compute graph); a formula is a scalar, the gradient is by cell, and the values at the integration points are not derived. The second view of a comparison shows the contours only (no cut, iso-surface, glyph, streamline or probe); a comparison is within the results opened (another step, another field or part), not with another file; the difference of two meshes is refused when they do not correspond, never interpolated. The volume is resampled on the CPU in the engine (the grid once, its values at each step); its transfer function is chosen among presets (the state accepts any points); it is not deformed, and is drawn over the surfaces inside it (the march does not stop at the depth of the scene); it is not lit, not shown in the second view of a comparison.

Testing

Section titled “Testing”

npx nx run editor-plugin-simulation:test: the numbers drawn (components, smoothing at the points, the missing cells kept missing whatever the smoothing, the counts out of range), the displacements of the vertices and the wire, the factors, the time axes, the state kept and what it shows; in the engine, the deformation and its bounds, the steps read one after the other without skipping one, a mode swinging, the probes read at the step and over the steps, drawn and their curves, the cut, the iso-surfaces and iso-lines drawn then the skin again; the provider (the entries by the numbers of the solver, no value between two steps nor on the wrong kind of entry, the series thinned, the extremes); the extraction (the tetrahedra of each cell, the sections of each type of cell, the cells without a value left out, the slice constant by cell, the skin clipped, the iso-lines and their deformation); the flow (the locator and a linear field read exactly, its gradient, the glyphs bounded and their arrows, a straight streamline through a uniform field, a helix on its circle and its ribbon twisted, the ends at a missing value, at no speed, outside, at their length), the glyphs and streamlines drawn by the engine; the derived fields (the principal values and their invariants, a linear gradient read exactly, the formulas read and their errors, the refusals and their reasons, the difference and its missing values), the derived fields and the side by side in the engine (the layers, the second view, its legend); the volume (its grid and its centering, a linear field read exactly, a field by cell as read, the samples missing and outside, the atlas, the transfer function, the cut in the grid, the graph compiled), drawn by the engine and cut.
