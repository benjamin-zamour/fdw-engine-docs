@fdw/editor-plugin-render-studio

Stage a product for its renders on the viewer: the staging apart from the model, the named cameras, their framings, their output formats and their gate; its lighting; the materials of a library assigned to its elements; its preview, its final renders, their files, their queue and its sequences.
Product Editor
Visibility Public: Meant to be used by applications and plugins.
Domains The editor, Rendering
Dependencies editor-ui, engine-dataviz, material-library, project, render-studio
Dependents None
Tests test-unit (npx nx run editor-plugin-render-studio:<target>, the families of the CI in Testing)
API API reference
Source editor/plugins/editor-plugin-render-studio

The render studio plugin (fdw.renderStudio, design: specs/design/p30-render-studio.md, steps 1 to 7). It adds the Cameras, Lighting, Materials, Render and Queue panels to the viewer in the render-studio profile: the staging of a product apart from its model (@fdw/render-studio), its named cameras made from the view or from framings (three-quarter, front, back, left, right, top, detail of the selection), their output sizes and focal lengths. Looking through a camera puts the view where it is and shows its gate: the outside of the image darkened, the field of the camera that of the gate (level.camera.set, level.camera.gate of the protocol of @fdw/editor-ui). The lighting of the staging (rigs, lights placed around the model, environment, background, ground) is the stage of the views (level.stage, level.stage.panorama). The materials of a library of the material editor (@fdw/material-library) are assigned to the elements selected (an instance path, the source key the engine finds for it): read from the library (an instance on its parent), given to the views (level.materials), which draw them on the element and what is under it; the panel says a broken link, an element not in the model, a material not drawn. The preview draws the view by the path tracer (the path rendering of the level, level.rendering), which refines while nothing moves and stops at its samples (level.preview.samples: 64, 256 by default, 1024, or without end); the status bar says the samples gathered (level.preview), or why the view stays rasterized (a backend that traces no rays: WebGL2). The final render renders the camera looked through at its output size (level.render), by tiles, each until the first of its criteria (the samples, a duration shared by the tiles, a noise left), then denoised whole (the à-trous filter, its strength; 0 for none); the Render panel (at the bottom) sets them, says where it is (level.render.progress, also at the right of the status bar), stops and resumes it at its tile, shows its image (level.render.image, a PNG, with its alpha). Its files (specs/design/p30-render-studio.md §12): a usage (web: JPEG at 85, 72 dpi; catalog: PNG 8 bits, 300 dpi; print: PNG 16 bits; retouching: OpenEXR with all the passes) or settings of its own (the format, its bit depth, quality or pixel type, the density, the passes: albedo, normal, depth, objects, mask), encoded by the engine from the linear image (level.render.save, level.render.files); named by a pattern ({staging}-{camera}, the pass added), never over a file already there; written in the folder chosen for the session (HostFiles.openFolder), or each where asked where the platform gives no folder; the metadata in each file, a .render.json record beside (the staging, the settings). The queue (specs/design/p30-render-studio.md §13, the Queue panel at the bottom): jobs rendered one after the other, each with the render and output settings it was queued with and the staging as it was then (a camera, all the cameras, or a sequence); its images rendered by the final render one by one, their files written in the folder; paused (the render stopped at its tile, resumed there), a job cancelled (its files kept), failed and retried from its image; kept with the layout (a job rendering when the editor closed is paused at its image). The sequences of the staging: a turntable (a camera going round its target, the lights turning with it: the product turns under the lights of the studio) or a camera path (through cameras, around their targets, eased); their frames shown by a cursor (the time cursor of @fdw/engine-dataviz, played at their images per second); numbered files ({frame}, -0001 added when the name has none) and one record for all.

Both demo hosts provide it in the profile:

Command What it does
fdw.renderStudio.new An empty staging for the model opened
fdw.renderStudio.open A .staging.json file read (or the { name, data } file given)
fdw.renderStudio.save The staging written, named after its model
fdw.renderStudio.addCamera A camera where the view is
fdw.renderStudio.addFraming A framing (kind, 'selection' for the parts selected)
fdw.renderStudio.lookThrough Looks through a camera, by its id
fdw.renderStudio.previousCamera, next The cameras in turn
fdw.renderStudio.freeCamera The gate hidden, the view free
fdw.renderStudio.applyRig A rig ('three-point', 'lightbox', 'white-background')
fdw.renderStudio.choosePanorama An HDR panorama chosen (or the file given): the environment
fdw.renderStudio.defaultLight The lighting removed: the default light of the viewer
fdw.renderStudio.openLibrary A library of materials given, or a folder of the user
fdw.renderStudio.assignMaterial A material (its GUID or path, or the one chosen) to the selection
fdw.renderStudio.unassignMaterial An assignment removed (by its id), or those of the selection
fdw.renderStudio.preview The preview by the path tracer turned on, or off
fdw.renderStudio.previewSamples The samples of the preview (a number, 0 without end)
fdw.renderStudio.renderCamera A final render of the camera looked through (settings given kept)
fdw.renderStudio.stopRender The render stopped after its batch of frames
fdw.renderStudio.resumeRender The render stopped resumed at its tile
fdw.renderStudio.saveRender The files of the render written (output settings given kept)
fdw.renderStudio.renderFolder The folder of the renders chosen (or the HostFolder given)
fdw.renderStudio.addSequence A turntable of the camera looked through ('path': all cameras)
fdw.renderStudio.queueCameras The camera looked through queued (ids, or 'all'; settings)
fdw.renderStudio.queueSequence A sequence queued, by its id (the first one; settings)
fdw.renderStudio.startQueue The jobs waiting or paused rendered, in the folder
fdw.renderStudio.pauseQueue The queue paused: its render stopped at its tile
fdw.renderStudio.clearQueue The jobs done, failed or cancelled removed

The staging is kept with the layout. A model opened again, the camera looked through is put back once its geometry is loaded (the viewer frames a model it opens). The panorama of an environment is named in the staging, its bytes are not: given with the staging to open ({ name, data, panorama: { name, data } }, as the web editor does from beside the staging), or chosen again; the panels say when it is missing. So is the library of the materials: given to openLibrary (the web editor writes ?library= in memory), or a folder opened again. The samples of the preview are kept with the layout too (the context key renderStudio.previewSamples); the web editor turns the preview on with ?preview, and sets its samples with ?samples=. The settings of the final renders are kept with the layout too (samples, seconds, noise, denoise, tileSize; the state of the last render in the context key renderStudio.render); the web editor renders the camera looked through once the model is loaded with ?render= (its samples). The output settings are kept with the layout (output); the folder is not (a handle of the platform), asked again in a new session. The queue is kept with the layout (queue; its state in the context key renderStudio.queue: idle, running, pausing); the web editor queues the first sequence of the staging once the model is loaded and renders it in a folder in memory with ?queue= (its samples).
