Source labs/demo-scenes

The scenes of the demos, independent of where they run: the routes of the web demo (labs/demo, in a canvas) and the native demo (labs/demo-native, in a native window). A scene is created from a RenderBackend and a Surface, and renders a frame at a time:

It also holds the geometry the scenes share: createSphere, createCube, axisAngle.

The games: the maze, the first person shooter (createFpsScene), the third-person game in the forest (createTpsScene, with a physics engine and a resource store holding the models of apps/docs/public/models/forest, made by tools/assets).
