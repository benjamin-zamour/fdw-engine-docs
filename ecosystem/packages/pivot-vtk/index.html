@fdw/pivot-vtk

The reader of the files of VTK (legacy, .vtu, .vtp, and their series) of the pivot model: the results of a simulation, read on demand.
Product Ecosystem
Visibility Public: Meant to be used by applications and plugins.
Domains Procedural and simulation, Resources, assets and formats
Dependencies capabilities, pivot, pivot-results
Dependents editor-ui
Tests test-unit (npx nx run pivot-vtk:<target>, the families of the CI in Testing)

Source conversion/pivot-vtk

The reader of the files of VTK of the pivot model (@fdw/pivot): the results of a simulation, into the namespace of @fdw/pivot-results, their values read on demand. Design: design/p31-simulation.md (in French), step 2; the tutorial « Reading simulation results » of the documentation.

TypeScript only, for the web and Node: it depends on @fdw/pivot, @fdw/pivot-results and @fdw/capabilities. The zlib blocks are inflated by DecompressionStream.

Usage

Section titled “Usage”

What it reads

Section titled “What it reads”
  • The legacy format (.vtk, # vtk DataFile Version 2 to 5.1), in text or in binary: unstructured grids and polygonal data; SCALARS, VECTORS, NORMALS, TENSORS, TENSORS6, TEXTURE_COORDINATES, COLOR_SCALARS, FIELD, GLOBAL_IDS, PEDIGREE_IDS; the cells as a list (before 5) or as offsets and connectivity (5.1).
  • The XML formats (.vtu, .vtp): in text, in base64 or appended (raw or base64), compressed by zlib or not, headers of 32 or 64 bits, little or big endian, in several pieces; the names of the components.
  • The series: the JSON of ParaView (.vtu.series, .vtk.series) and the collections (.pvd), whose parts are meshes of the same result set.

A file is one step (its time from TimeValue if it has one, else an index); a series one step a file. The points, the cells and the descriptions of the arrays are read first, from the first step; the values of a field at a step when asked (readResource, through FieldCache), from the file of its step: the reader keeps the two files parsed last. The arrays GLOBAL_IDS, GlobalNodeId and GlobalElementId (or those named by pointIds, cellIds) become the numbers of the points and cells, not fields.

What it says

Section titled “What it says”

VTK has no units: meters and Z up are assumed and said (units, defaulted), unless frame is given; the units of the fields are those of units, or unknown, and said (field-units). A color table of the file is a line (lost): the palettes are the viewer’s. The values of a step whose file does not have the field, or whose mesh is not the one of the first step, come back missing, with a line. A file that is not VTK, or a dataset it does not read, is a PivotError (invalid).

Public API

Section titled “Public API”
Export Description
registerVtk, vtkPivotManifest, vtkPivotPlugin The plugin fdw.vtk (pivot.reader, on demand), in a registry
vtkPivotReader, VtkReaderOptions, vtkStage The reader: the frame, the units of the fields, the axis, the arrays of ids
parseVtk, parseLegacy, parseXmlDataset, VtkDataset A file parsed: its points and cells, its arrays read when asked
parseSeries, SeriesEntry The files of a series and their times
VtkSyntaxError Where a file stops being VTK

Limits

Section titled “Limits”

Structured points, structured and rectilinear grids, the polyhedra (their faces), the LZ4 and LZMA compressors, the multiblock datasets (.vtm) and the HDF5 format of VTK are not read. A mesh that changes from one step to the next is refused at the steps where it changed. No writer.

Testing

Section titled “Testing”

npx vitest run --config conversion/pivot-vtk/vitest.config.mts: the files of src/fixtures/, written by VTK 9 itself (scripts/make-fixtures.py, with pip install vtk): the same mesh of mixed cells in every encoding of both formats, read and compared with what VTK put in them (expected.json); polygonal data in the order of VTK; a series step by step, with a field missing at a step; a collection of two parts; a mesh that changes; the lines of the report. tutorial.spec.ts reads the cantilever of the documentation (apps/docs/public/models/cantilever/, written by scripts/make-cantilever.mjs).
