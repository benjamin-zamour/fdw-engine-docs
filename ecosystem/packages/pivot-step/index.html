Source geometry/pivot-step

The reader of STEP files (ISO 10303-21; AP203, AP214, AP242) of the pivot model (@fdw/pivot): the exact B-reps of the bodies, the product structure and its instances, colors, units, and graphical PMI. A plugin of the registry of the pivot, beside the plugin of glTF (@fdw/engine-gltf). Design: specs/design/p22-pivot.md (in French), step 5; the tutorial « Reading a STEP file » of the documentation.

It depends on @fdw/pivot, @fdw/capabilities and @fdw/geometry-kernel (its projections, to place the edges on their curves and the curves on their faces): TypeScript only, no GPU, no OpenCascade. A STEP writer and reading on demand are out of its scope for now.

Usage

Section titled “Usage”

In the engine, installPivot(store, registry, { kernel }) of @fdw/engine-pivot loads .step files as models.

What it reads

Section titled “What it reads”
  • The bodies: MANIFOLD_SOLID_BREP, BREP_WITH_VOIDS, FACETED_BREP, SHELL_BASED_SURFACE_MODEL; a brep resource each, its elements with their STEP instance as source key (#42). The curves: line, circle, ellipse, B-spline (rational, Bézier, uniform), polyline, the 3D curve of a curve on surfaces. The surfaces: plane, cylinder, cone, sphere, torus, extrusion, revolution, B-spline (rational), the basis of a trimmed one. An edge goes along its curve (range[0] < range[1]), its curve turned over when the file’s goes the other way; the outer loop of a plane is the one the file marks, or the largest. The pcurves of the file are not read: they are found from the 3D curves, the truth, where they are straight in the parameters (the seams of the analytic surfaces); a whole sphere bounded by a vertex gets its seam; a whole torus its loop turned counterclockwise.
  • The structure: a product definition is a node, a prototype when it is a component, placed by an instance node for each occurrence (NEXT_ASSEMBLY_USAGE_OCCURRENCE and its contextual placement, ITEM_DEFINED_TRANSFORMATION or a Cartesian operator, whichever side the component is on); the mapped items, as instances of their representation; a file without products, its representations as roots. A part of one body carries its B-rep; several, a child node each.
  • The names and properties: the names of the products and of the occurrences; partNumber, description, revision; the header as properties of the model (author, organization, originatingSystem, preprocessor, timeStamp, schema, description).
  • The colors: the surface colors and transparencies of the styled items, on a solid (brep.appearance), a shell or a face (face.appearance); sRGB in the file, linear in the pivot.
  • The graphical PMI: text literals and polylines, trimmed/composite curves and circles or ellipses in presentation representations, attached to their product node in its units. Their annotation kind is inferred from AP242 entity types; geometric-item associations resolve to B-reps, faces and edges when the relation names both the annotation and the STEP geometry item.
  • The units: the frame of the model in the length unit of its first representation (metersPerUnit), Z up; a representation in other units scaled by its node. The tolerance of each B-rep is the uncertainty of its context.

What it says

Section titled “What it says”

Never a silent loss. A body it cannot read (a surface it does not know) is a line, lost, and the rest of the file is read. Unsupported PMI entities and semantic fields are reported by type; the reader does not claim complete AP242 semantic PMI support. What it does not use is said by type of entity, but for the contexts, categories, approvals and people, which carry nothing of the model. The colors of curves and of instances in an assembly, a representation without units (millimeters assumed), a component without placement are lines. A file that is not ISO 10303-21 is a PivotError (invalid) with its line.

Public API

Section titled “Public API”
Export Description
registerStep, stepPivotManifest, stepPivotPlugin The plugin fdw.step (pivot.reader, .step, .stp, .p21), in a registry
stepPivotReader, StepReaderOptions, stepStage The reader; the tolerance of the representations that declare none
parseStep, StepFile, StepInstance, StepEntity The clear text of ISO 10303-21: its header, its instances, simple or complex
StepValue, StepRef, StepEnum, StepTyped, StepBinary, derived, StepSyntaxError Its values, and the error that says where a file is not ISO 10303-21

Limits

Section titled “Limits”

The B-reps are read whole, not on demand; the colors of instances in an assembly (contextual styles), layers, PMI semantics beyond the supported graphical subset, validation properties and external references are not read (and said). Each face of a body is read once: a face shared by two shells with opposite orientations is copied. The basic kernel tessellates the curved faces whose parameters span a rectangle; the others are tessellated by OpenCascade (@fdw/geometry-kernel-occt).

Testing

Section titled “Testing”

npx vitest run --config geometry/pivot-step/vitest.config.mts: the parser (the escapes of the strings, the complex instances, the errors with their line); solids written by OpenCascade (box, cylinder, cone, sphere, torus, a box with a hole, every body of the kernel battery) read back and measured by OpenCascade, tessellated again by the basic kernel; an assembly of OpenCascade’s XCAF (names, instances, nested placements, colors, transparency); inches; a faceted solid; graphical PMI text, trimmed curves and a target face association; the lines of what is not read, the strict mode. tutorial.spec.ts reads the roller of the documentation (apps/docs/public/models/roller.step, written by scripts/make-roller.mjs); the end of the tutorial, tessellated and written in glTF, is tested by @fdw/engine-pivot (step-tutorial.spec.ts): the writer of glTF is of the engine.
