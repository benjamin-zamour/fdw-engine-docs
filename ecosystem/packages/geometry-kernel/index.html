@fdw/geometry-kernel

The geometric kernel: an abstract interface over the B-reps of the pivot model, its capabilities in plugins.
Product Ecosystem
Visibility Public: Meant to be used by applications and plugins.
Domains Geometry
Dependencies capabilities, pivot
Dependents editor-ui, engine-pivot, geometry-kernel-occt, pivot-brep, pivot-step
Tests test-unit (npx nx run geometry-kernel:<target>, the families of the CI in Testing)
API API reference
Source geometry/geometry-kernel

The geometric kernel: an abstract interface that operates on the B-reps of the pivot model (@fdw/pivot), each of its capabilities provided by a plugin declared by its manifest (@fdw/capabilities), the plugins and the bodies in a host (a worker), only ids crossing. With the basic kernel, in TypeScript: the primitives, the similarities, the extrusion and the revolution of resolved profiles, the tessellation. Design: specs/design/p23-geometry-kernel.md (in French); the tutorials « The geometric kernel » and « Writing a capability » of the documentation.

Pure TypeScript, without GPU and without the engine: it depends on @fdw/capabilities and @fdw/pivot.

Section titled “Public API”
Export Description
GeometryKernel The kernel of its users: primitive, transform, extrude, revolve, tessellate, run (any capability), adopt, brep, release, manifests, provides; local(setup)
KernelHost Where the plugins run and the bodies live: register, run, adopt, brep, release, handle
localChannel, portChannel, serveKernel, KernelPort A host in the page; behind a port (a worker, a MessageChannel), the arrays of the tessellations transferred
KernelError, KernelErrorCode The typed error, the same on both sides of a worker: missing, load, invalid-input, degenerate, invalid-profile, invalid-brep, unsupported, unknown-body, failed
kernelPrimitives, kernelTransform, kernelExtrude, kernelRevolve, kernelTessellate The capabilities 1.0.0 and their details (the shapes of the primitives, the surfaces tessellated)
KernelOperations, KernelPlugin, KernelContext The contract of a plugin: an operation by capability, augmented by the capabilities to come; the tolerance shared by all
PrimitiveInput, Profile, ProfileSegment, ExtrudeInput, RevolveInput The inputs: the primitives in their placement; the profiles resolved (lines, arcs, NURBS; the outer loop, then its holes)
Tessellation, TessellationCriteria, screenTolerance The triangles by face, the edges as polylines; the two geometric criteria; the tolerance of half a pixel at a distance
registerBasicKernel, basicKernelManifest, BrepBuilder The basic kernel, in TypeScript; the builder of the B-reps of a plugin
tessellateModel, tessellationMesh The meshes of the B-reps of a pivot model, derived from them (derivedFrom), by face, with the colors of the faces
conformanceCases, runConformance, checkTessellation The battery of pathological cases a plugin passes; a mesh closed, facing outward, its volume
compareKernels, ComparisonResult A kernel compared with a reference (OpenCascade, @fdw/geometry-kernel-occt) on the bodies of the battery: volumes and areas within 1 %, each body read by the other
checkPrimitive, checkExtrude, checkRevolve, checkProfile, checkTransform, checkTessellate The checks of the inputs, part of the contract: the same input, the same typed error, whatever the plugin
curvePoint, surfacePoint, surfaceNormal, sampleCurve, matrices The evaluation of the curves and the surfaces of the pivot; the matrices 4×4 column-major
curveParameter, surfaceParameters, loopParameters, signedArea The projections: the parameter of a point on a curve, its parameters on a surface (and the one a pole does not determine); the parameters of a loop of a face, continuous; the turn of a polygon of parameters

Limits

Section titled “Limits”

The basic kernel tessellates the planar faces with their holes, and the curved faces whose parameters span a rectangle: those it makes, and most of those of a file. The pcurves that a face lacks (read from a file) are found from the points of its edges (loopParameters: the angles unwrapped along the loop, a pole taking the parameter of its neighbor, the two passes of a seam a period apart). A curved face with holes, or whose parameters are not a rectangle, is unsupported until the general triangulation in parameters. OpenCascade, the reference of the battery (step 2), is in @fdw/geometry-kernel-occt; the verification and the repair, the sweep, the booleans and the fillets are the next steps.

Testing

Section titled “Testing”

npx vitest run --config geometry/geometry-kernel/vitest.config.mts: the battery on the basic kernel, the kernel behind MessageChannel ports as in workers, the topology of the bodies (each edge used twice, once each way), their tessellations within their tolerance.
