Scenes and formats

@fdw/engine-scene-io saves the scenes and loads them: whole levels, pieces placed in other scenes, files of other tools. Everything goes through one representation, SceneData: importers make it, exporters write it, captureScene and instantiateScene link it to a Scene. The tutorial saves and loads a scene, Writing an importer loads a format of its own.

Export What it is
SceneData, SceneDataType A scene as plain data; its resource type
captureScene A branch of a scene as SceneData
instantiateScene, SceneInstance The nodes of a SceneData in a scene, with its resources
ComponentCodec, SceneCodecs, numericCodec How the components are written and read
sceneCodecs, meshRendererCodec, lightCodec, … The codecs of engine-scene
lodCodec The levels of detail of a SceneLods
writeFdwScene, parseFdwScene, fdwSceneLoader The format of the engine: write, read, load
installSceneIo Registers its loader in a store
SceneExporter, ExportedFile What an exporter is: SceneData in, files out
migrateFdwScene, fdwSceneMigrations The versions of the format and their migrations
modelPaths, scenePaths, resolveMember The paths of the nodes of the instances
sceneAddresses, modelAddresses, findMember The ids and paths of the nodes of an instance, nested

The packages give the codecs of their components: physicsCodec (@fdw/engine-physics), soundEmitterCodec (@fdw/engine-audio), behaviorCodec (@fdw/engine-behavior). @fdw/engine-gltf reads glTF as a scene (installGltfScenes) and writes it (writeGltf).

SceneData

Section titled “SceneData”
Field What it holds
nodes SceneNodeData: the parents before their children
batches SceneBatchData: many leaf nodes alike, their transforms in flat arrays
meshes The meshes written with the scene (MeshData): #mesh/<index>
materials The materials written with the scene (MaterialData): #material/<index>

A node has id (its stable id), name, position, rotation (x y z w), scale, visible, static, occluder, layers (the defaults are left out), parent (an index), and components: by name, { version, data }. An instance makes it the root of a model (model) or of a scene (scene), with its overrides. parentIds and parentPath place it under a node of the instance parent.

The entities are numbered for the references between them: the nodes, then the entities of each batch in turn. The data of the components may hold typed arrays (a heightfield): the formats write them as binary.

A reference to a resource (ResourceRef) is a URI, relative to the scene or with its scheme, or { uri, options } when it is loaded with options (a texture in a color space).

Writing: captureScene

Section titled “Writing: captureScene”
Option Default Description
store The store whose values are written by URI (store.identify)
codecs sceneCodecs The codecs of the components
root the root The branch written: the descendants of this node
uri Where the scene will be written: the references are relative to it
instances 'reference' The instances by their source and changes, or 'expand'ed as nodes
batchMin 16 Leaf nodes alike under a parent, at least this many, become a batch
warn console.warn What could not be written

A mesh or a material made by the store is written by its URI. The others are written with the scene: the CPU data of a mesh; the graph of a material, its values, its features and its render state (a graph material, kind: 'graph'). Its textures must have a URI: a texture made by code (baked, rendered) is defined in the store under one (defineTexture), and defined again before the scene is loaded. The meshes and materials of a scene file are written again with the scene saved, never referenced.

Reading: instantiateScene

Section titled “Reading: instantiateScene”
Option Default Description
store (required) The store the resources are loaded from, kept until dispose()
parent the root Where the instance goes
codecs sceneCodecs The codecs of the components
uri the handle’s The URI the relative references are resolved against
wrap true Under a node of its own (root), or straight under the parent
signal Cancels: the nodes made are removed
warn console.warn What could not be read

The instance makes the nodes in order, its models and scenes loaded together, then its batches, then reads the components phase after phase (codec.phase), the codecs of a phase together. Given a handle, the instance is known by the URI of its scene: captured again, it is written as a reference. entities gives the entity of each index.

Codecs

Section titled “Codecs”
Member Description
name, version The component, by its name in its world; the version written with the data
write(e, ctx) The data of an entity, or undefined for none
read(d, e, ctx) Makes the component again; may be asynchronous (resources)
migrate(v, d) Brings data of the version v to the current one
phase Read after the lower phases (0 by default): lod reads in phase 1
has(e) For the data kept outside the components of the world (the behavior systems)

WriteContext: resource(value), mesh(mesh), material(material) (by URI, or written with the scene), indexOf(entity), warn. ReadContext: load(type, ref), mesh(ref), material(ref), entity(index), service(key), warn.

defineComponent(name, schema, { defaults, version, persist }): a component of numbers with persist: true is written field by field, by numericCodec. Without a codec, a component is not written; read without its codec (or of a newer version), its data is kept on the entity and written again as it was.

Codec Component Data
meshRendererCodec mesh renderer mesh, material, castShadow
lightCodec light The light as it is: colors, attenuation, cone, shadows
cameraViewCodec camera view The projection
lodCodec(lods) lod The levels: the entities of each, their distance
physicsCodec(physics) physics body The description of the body (NodeBodyDesc) or of the character
soundEmitterCodec(a) sound emitter The sound of a node: its source by URI, its options
behaviorCodec(systems) behavior The graphs run for the entity, their variables

The userData of the bodies is not written; a sound made by code neither. The animators belong to the code of the characters: the animation graphs are resources (AnimationGraphType), loaded by it.

Instances and their changes

Section titled “Instances and their changes”

An instance of a model (instantiate) or of a scene keeps what it was made from (components model instance, scene instance). Captured, it is written as its source and its overrides: what differs from what the source makes, by the path of the node from the root of the instance — the names of the nodes (trunk/leaves), #k for the k-th child when the names repeat, name#i for the child holding the i-th primitive of a mesh. An override replaces fields or components, or removes the node (removed: true) with its descendants. The nodes added under a node of an instance are written as nodes, with their parentPath.

Stable ids

Section titled “Stable ids”

A path breaks when a node of the source is renamed or moved. So the nodes carry a stable id (@fdw/engine-scene: stableIdOf, setStableId, ensureStableId; a random UUID, the format of the ids of the pivot), written as id in SceneNodeData and kept through the edits. An override finds its node by ids, the chain of the ids traversed from the root of the instance, and keeps its path as the fallback: when the ids are not found any more, the path is used and a warning says so.

The chain goes through the instances inside the instance: here the lamp placed on the desk (9a1e…, a node of the desk), then its bulb (c27b…, a node of the lamp). The changes the desk makes to its own lamp are part of what the desk makes: the level writes only its own. A node added under a node of a nested instance has its parentIds and its parentPath.

captureScene writes the ids the nodes have; with ids: true, it gives one to those without (what the editor saves). The nodes of an instance have the ids of their source (the same in each instance of it: scoped by the root of the instance, tagged instance root). A model gives the ids of its nodes when its data has them (ModelNodeData.id, from the pivot of P22); otherwise its nodes are found by their path.

The instance path of a node, the ids of the roots of the instances around it then its own (instancePathOf, resolveInstancePath), names it in the whole scene, the same after it is saved and read again: the selection of the editor keeps it (see Editing).

The format of the engine

Section titled “The format of the engine”

writeFdwScene(data, path, { binary, indent }) gives the JSON and its binary (.bin beside it, or binary: 'embedded': base64 in the JSON). The JSON is the SceneData, with format: "fdw-scene", schemaVersion, buffers, and the graphs of the materials written once in graphs. Each typed array becomes { "$binary": { "type": "f32", "byteOffset": 0, "length": 60 } }, aligned on 8 bytes.

The loader (installSceneIo) provides the meshes and materials as sub-resources of the file (level.scene.json#mesh/0), shared by the instances, reloaded with the file.

Versions and migrations

Section titled “Versions and migrations”

Each format has its version, apart from the version of the engine: schemaVersion of the scenes, version of the graphs (material, animation, behavior), the version of each codec. A document of an older version goes through its migrations, n → n + 1, until the current one: fdwSceneMigrations, materialGraphMigrations, animationGraphMigrations, behaviorGraphMigrations, codec.migrate. A newer one is refused with a clear message.

The graphs are resources too: MaterialGraphType (registered by installAssets), AnimationGraphType and BehaviorGraphType (their loaders, animationGraphLoader and behaviorGraphLoader, to register).

glTF

Section titled “glTF”

installGltfScenes(store) loads the .gltf and .glb as SceneDataType: the nodes, the meshes (the sub-resources of the model), the lights (KHR_lights_punctual), the cameras, the bodies (KHR_physics_rigid_bodies, KHR_implicit_shapes), the instances of EXT_mesh_gpu_instancing as batches, and the components of FDW_scene.

writeGltf(data, path, { store, uri, binary, warn }) writes a .glb (or a .gltf and its .bin): the meshes de-interleaved, the images copied as they are, the lights as a color and an intensity, the batches as EXT_mesh_gpu_instancing. What glTF has no place for — the graph of a material, the exact light, a heightfield, the other components — goes to FDW_scene, which other tools ignore: the engine reads the file back without loss. glTF has no references: capture with instances: 'expand'.

Stable identifiers

Section titled “Stable identifiers”

The scenes refer to the resources by their URI. For references that survive a file moved, mount a CatalogSource under a scheme: asset:8d42c7e1 reads the path the catalog gives, set a new one and what is loaded reloads.

Archives

Section titled “Archives”

A game ships its files in a zip, read by an ArchiveSource mounted under the files of development. Its ZipReader refuses an archive past its limits, all of them options: maxEntries (65 535), maxEntrySize (1 GiB) and maxTotalSize (4 GiB); an entry is never inflated past the size it declares.
