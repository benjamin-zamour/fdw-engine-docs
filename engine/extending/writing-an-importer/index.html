Writing an importer

A level drawn in text, one character per cell: # a wall, g a gem, S the start, . the floor. Its importer is a loader of SceneData, registered in the store like the others: the level then loads as a glTF or a scene of the engine would, with its progress, its cancellation, its meshes shared.

…

The importer: a loader of SceneData

Section titled “The importer: a loader of SceneData”

An importer reads the bytes of a file and gives a SceneData: nodes, batches, and the references to their meshes and materials. It is a ResourceLoader of SceneDataType; the store chooses it by the extension of the file, as it chooses the loader of glTF or of the scenes of the engine.

An error of the file is a ResourceError('decode', …): the handle of the scene fails with it, its message tells the row and the column.

Meshes and materials

Section titled “Meshes and materials”

The meshes and the materials of the format are sub-resources of its file: the loader provides their data (MeshDataType, MaterialDataType) under an id, and the nodes refer to them by #mesh/<id> and #material/<id>. Two levels using the same mesh share it; a reload of the file updates them. A material is data too: pbr or phong like those of glTF and OBJ, or graph, a material graph and its values.

A reference can also be the URI of another file, relative to the level: "../models/fir.glb#mesh/0/0", or a whole model as an instance (instance: { model: '../models/fir.glb' }).

Nodes and batches

Section titled “Nodes and batches”

A node has the fields of a node of the engine (name, position, rotation, scale, visible, static, occluder, layers), a parent (its index, before it), and its components by name, each with the version of its codec. The walls are a batch: a mesh, a material, a transform each in flat arrays, made in one call by scene.createBatch.

The entities are numbered for the references between them (a parent, the levels of detail): the nodes first, then the entities of each batch in turn.

The components of your game

Section titled “The components of your game”

gem is a component of the game. Its codec reads what the importer wrote into the component, and writes it back when the scene is saved: the level is then saved as a scene of the engine, gems and all.

A component of numbers only can do without: defined with persist: true, it is read and written field by field. A codec is for the others: references to resources (ctx.load, ctx.mesh, ctx.material), to entities (ctx.entity(index), ctx.indexOf(entity)), objects to make again. phase orders the codecs that need the components of others; migrate brings the data of an older version to the current one.

Exporting

Section titled “Exporting”

An exporter is the other way: a SceneExporter takes a SceneData and gives files. writeFdwScene (the format of the engine) and writeGltf (glTF) are the two of the engine; one of yours writes your format.
