Section views

A section view removes a part of the model to show its inside. The engine cuts in the shaders, on the four backends, without changing the meshes: instantly, on any model, with or without a B-rep. Where a plane goes through a closed volume, the slice is filled, here hatched, by the stencil.

Planes and sections

Section titled “Planes and sections”

A plane removes what is in front of its normal: the points p where dot(normal, p) > offset. Planes are grouped in sections:

  • the planes of a section are in union: the section removes the region where all its planes remove. One plane removes a half space, two a quarter, three a corner;
  • the sections are in intersection: a point stays where no section removes it. Six sections of one plane each keep the inside of a box.

clipping is an option of render and addScenePasses. Every pass of the scene clips: the color, the outputs (the identifiers: what is removed is not picked), the shadow maps (what is removed casts no shadow). A frame holds at most MaxClipPlanes (8) planes, all sections together; beyond, the renderer throws rather than ignore a plane. The programs are compiled for 1, 2, 4 or 8 planes: moving a plane recompiles nothing, adding planes may select another variant.

Materials with the feature clipping: false are not cut: the lines and the labels of @fdw/engine-draw (the dimensions stay visible), the planes themselves.

The slices

Section titled “The slices”

Where a plane cuts a closed volume, its inside is visible: the renderer fills it. For each plane, after the opaque items, it counts in the stencil the faces of the clipped items behind the plane (an odd count: the plane is inside a volume), then draws the plane there, masked where the other sections remove or the other planes of its section keep. The depth of the pass is then depth24plus-stencil8; a depth given to addScenePasses must have the stencil too.

slices: false leaves a section open. The stencil supposes closed volumes: a mesh with holes, or one crossing another, gives wrong slices where its faces miss. The slices need the items on the CPU list (not those of a GpuScene culled on the GPU), and 8 planes at most are filled.

The planes, seen

Section titled “The planes, seen”

SectionPlanes of @fdw/engine-draw draws the planes where they cross the bounds of the model: a transparent polygon (opacity 0 to 1; at 0, it is invisible and still cuts) and its border.

In the view state

Section titled “In the view state”

The sections of an application are objects of the view state: named, enabled or not, saved with it. addSectionBox(min, max) creates the six sections of a box in a gesture; the limits of the state (those of the profile, never more than the renderer draws) refuse what would exceed them.

The exact section, the curves of the cut computed on the B-rep, measurable and exportable, will come with the geometric kernel.
