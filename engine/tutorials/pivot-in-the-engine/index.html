The pivot in the engine

A reader of the pivot model reads a file into memory; @fdw/engine-pivot makes it a model of the engine, loaded by the store and placed by instantiate as a glTF would be. The other way, a scene of the engine becomes a pivot model, which any writer writes. Here, an assembly in a format of our own, in millimeters and Z up, rendered three times, then exported to STL.

…

The reader

Section titled “The reader”

A part is a prototype, a box whose six faces are those of a B-rep (faces, two triangles each); each place is an instance of it. The colors are appearances, CAD’s: on a part, or on one of its faces. The steel is a material of the pivot: a material graph of the engine and its values.

Writing a reader tells the rest: the manifest, the report, the reading on demand.

Loading it in the engine

Section titled “Loading it in the engine”

installPivot registers in the store a loader of ModelData for the extensions of the readers of a registry. A file of these formats then loads as a glTF or an OBJ: ModelType, its meshes and materials realized, instantiate to place it.

The loader reads the file with its reader, validates the model, then adapts it (adaptPivot):

  • The frame: a model in millimeters and Z up is placed under a node that converts it to the engine’s frame (meters, Y up). The data are not touched: the precision of the file is kept.
  • The instances: each occurrence of a prototype is a node of the engine; the four bolts share one mesh and one material, drawn in one call by the instancing. Their stable id is the path of ids from the instancing node: the changes of a saved scene find them again.
  • The appearances: the color of a face wins over the material of the primitive, which wins over the color of the part. One material per color, shared; a primitive whose faces have their own color is split by them (the top of the plate).
  • The materials are graph materials of the engine, their instances resolved; the textures of the file are sub-resources of the model, those beside it read by the store when a material is realized.
  • The losses (morph targets, a shear the nodes cannot hold) are lines of the report, given to the warn of the assets; with mode: 'strict', the loading fails at the first one.

What the engine keeps of the pivot

Section titled “What the engine keeps of the pivot”

The engine keeps neither the file nor the whole pivot model: its link, #pivot beside the model, keeps the structure: the nodes and their ids, the source keys, the properties, the extensions, the history of construction. Its meshes are deferred (their faces and materials kept, without their vertices), its textures read again by the store.

Exporting the scene

Section titled “Exporting the scene”

exportScene captures the scene (its instances expanded), makes it a pivot model, validates it and writes it with the writer of the file’s extension. The meshes and the materials are read from the store, as they were loaded; the textures by storeResolver. The nodes that came from a pivot model, found by their stable ids in the links, get back their source keys, their properties and their extensions.

What only the engine reads (the other components, the flags of the nodes, the shadows) is kept in the extensions of the nodes, under fdw.scene; a batch is a prototype and an instance per entity. The writer says what its format loses: STL keeps neither materials nor hierarchy, and its line says so.

scenePivot(data, { store }) does the middle step alone, from a SceneData captured by hand.
